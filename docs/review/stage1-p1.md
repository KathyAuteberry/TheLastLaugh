## "Why it works" drafts (2026-11-06), yes / no / edit each

18. **Graven Image I:** Tanks+healers always work on the west side and DPS on the east, so nobody needs a callout: Fire only decides spread or stack, and Ice only decides north or south on your own side. The fixed west-to-east beam line puts the 4 towers in a known row, so "the nth player without a beam takes the nth tower" resolves at a glance. The trap pass reuses the same split, so each holder has their own group to share the stack.
19. **Light of Judgment #1:** Nothing to resolve positionally. Ikuya times mitigation late in the cast so it also covers the Hyperdrive that follows immediately.
20. **Hyperdrive #1:** OT's Provoke on Ruin III #1 leaves OT holding aggro, so OT takes Hyperdrive with cooldowns still available. MT, who used the kitchen sink on Ruin III, supports with buddy mitigation.
21. **Graven Image II:** Tethers split by group, so in each round one group spreads while the other takes the gravity orb to the middle, and the groups swap for round 2. Round 1 resolves south and round 2 north, so the two Graviga puddles end up at opposite ends. Because round 2 is round 1 flipped, everyone has a mirrored pair of spread slots (MT↔D1, OT↔D2, H1↔D3, H2↔D4). The west/east trap pass reuses the group split, and the knockback carries each group into its own puddle: tanks+healers north, DPS south.
22. **Revolting Ruin III #2:** This mirrors Ruin III #1 with the tanks reversed. MT's mid-cast Provoke makes the retargeted 2nd hit land on OT again, so one OT invulnerability covers both hits.
23. **Light of Judgment #2:** Same as Light of Judgment #1: no positioning, and late mitigation also covers the Hyperdrive right after.
24. **Hyperdrive (no cast) #2:** MT holds aggro after provoking Ruin III #2, so MT takes Hyperdrive under their own invulnerability. Over Ruin III #2 and Hyperdrive #2, each tank uses exactly one invulnerability.
25. **Tele-trouncing:** Each arrow pair maps to one framed tile pair, so you find your spot by following your own arrows instead of waiting for callouts. The post-teleport spread is fixed by role: melee inside so everyone can keep hitting the boss, ranged outside, and no tethers need checking. The final spread and stack use fixed waymark spots (melee at 1–4, ranged on the cardinal edges; tanks+healers stack at 3, DPS at 1).
